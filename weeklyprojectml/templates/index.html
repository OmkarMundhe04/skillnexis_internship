📊 Dataset & Exploratory Data Analysis

Statistical properties and structural distributions from the benchmark loan dataset.

1. Target Class Balance

Finding: 68.7% approved (422 records) vs. 31.3% rejected (192 records). Stratified sampling was applied across all train/test splits to maintain identical distribution.

2. Credit History Impact

Finding: Applicants with good credit history (1.0) achieve a 79.6% approval rate. Applicants with adverse credit records (0.0) face a 91.8% rejection rate.

3. Feature Correlation Matrix

Finding: Notable collinearity (~0.62) exists between Total Household Income and requested Loan Amount, supporting the creation of normalized debt-to-income features.

    ⚖️ Ethical Considerations & Fair Lending Principles

    1. Protected Demographic Attributes: Variables like Gender and Married are present in historical benchmark datasets. In actual financial lending systems (e.g., under the US Equal Credit Opportunity Act - ECOA), using protected characteristics in credit decisions is illegal. Real-world scorecards must exclude protected traits.

    2. Historical Inequity: Algorithms risk learning and reinforcing past socio-economic biases present in historical training records. Independent fairness audits are vital.

    3. Adverse Action Transparency: Underwriting candidates denied credit are legally entitled to receive specific reasons for adverse action. Black-box models must be coupled with explainability tools (e.g. SHAP).

    4. Human-in-the-Loop Oversight: Algorithmic scores should remain advisory; final credit decisions require accredited human underwriting review.
